Out of fuel

Ran out of fuel?
Don't panic.

Stay safe, give us a call, and we'll get enough fuel to you to reach a filling station.

While you wait

  • Get the car as far out of the way as you safely can
  • Switch on your hazard lights
  • On a busy road, wait away from the car and behind a barrier if there is one
  • Send us a WhatsApp location pin if you're not sure where you are

How it works

  • Call or WhatsApp us with your location and fuel type
  • We agree the total price on the phone before we set off
  • We bring the fuel and help get you going again

Diesel that's run dry

Some diesel cars need the fuel system priming after running completely empty before they'll restart. If your car won't restart after refuelling, we can recover it to a garage.

Put the wrong fuel in?

Don't start the engine, and don't turn the ignition on. Starting it can cause expensive damage. Call us and we'll tell you your options, including recovering your vehicle to a garage.
